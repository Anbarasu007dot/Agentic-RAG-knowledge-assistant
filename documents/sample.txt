The Taj Mahal is one of the most famous monuments in the world.
It is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India.
The monument was built by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan.
He built it in memory of his beloved wife, Mumtaz Mahal.
Construction of the Taj Mahal began in 1632.
It took about 22 years to complete the monument.
Thousands of skilled workers and artisans contributed to its construction.
The Taj Mahal is made primarily of white marble.
Beautiful carvings and precious stones decorate its walls.
It stands on the southern bank of the Yamuna River.
The monument is known for its perfect symmetry and stunning architecture.
It attracts millions of tourists from around the world every year.
UNESCO recognized the Taj Mahal as a World Heritage Site in 1983.
It is considered a symbol of eternal love and architectural excellence.
Today, the Taj Mahal remains one of India's greatest cultural treasures.
